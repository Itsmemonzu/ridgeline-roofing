Good questions.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. No call-out fee and no obligation. You get photos and a written summary either way.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

If damage is limited to one area and the roof is under about 15 years old, a repair usually makes sense. We'll show you the evidence and give you both prices when both are reasonable.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes take one to two days. Larger, steeper or tiled roofs can take longer; your quote states the expected days.

Do you help with insurance claims?

We document the damage with photos and a written report, and can meet your adjuster on site.

Is the work guaranteed?

Every job carries our written workmanship warranty, on top of the manufacturer's material warranty. Terms are listed on your quote.
